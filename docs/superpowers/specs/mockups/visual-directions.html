Which overall look should Inbox Pilot have?

Three directions, each shown on the Today screen with your kind of data. Use the toggle on each to see it in dark mode. Click a card to pick it (you can also say "A's colors with B's type" in the terminal). Every icon is an inline SVG; there are no emoji anywhere.

Warm paper tones, a serif for headings and numbers, deep green accent. Calm and editorial, like a well-kept notebook.
